# LiftMaster
- official_url: https://www.liftmaster.com.au/
- origin: Australia (distributor/manufacturer) — see ownership note; the global "LiftMaster" brand itself originates in the USA as a Chamberlain Group brand (liftmaster.com), a separate site from the Australian one below
- ownership: **Naming overlap, two distinct entities — record clearly:**
  1. **liftmaster.com.au = Liftmaster Electronics Pty Ltd**, an independent Australian company established 1969 ("Liftmaster Electronics Pty Ltd, established in 1969, is a specialist provider of garage door and gate parking control systems"). It exclusively imports/distributes the Chamberlain "LiftMaster Professional" opener range in Australia, and separately designs/manufactures its own "Magic Button" electronic controls, PCBs, gate motors and sectional overhead doors in-house.
  2. **liftmaster.com (global)** is the US Chamberlain Group-owned LiftMaster brand — not the site used here; Perth customers encounter the brand through the Australian distributor above.
- founded: 1969 (Liftmaster Electronics Pty Ltd)
- product_lines: LiftMaster Professional range — LM60K (chain drive), LM60R (belt drive), LM1000K (chain drive), LM1000R (belt drive), domestic overhead/sectional openers; Magic Button electronic controls and PCBs (own-manufactured); swing/slide gate motors; barrier gates; sectional overhead garage doors

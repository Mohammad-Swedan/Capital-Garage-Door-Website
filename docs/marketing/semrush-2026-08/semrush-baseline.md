## What the leaders' rankings teach

1. **The generic + near-me cluster is where the traffic is, not the "perth" terms.**
   #1 site's top earners: garage door repair (5,400), garage door repairs (3,600),
   roller door repairs (2,900), near-me variants (1,300 ea). All localized SERPs, KD 17–28,
   and they win them with their HOMEPAGE.
2. **Homepage concentration works in this niche** — both leaders rank the homepage for ~everything.
   Our equivalent play: make `/garage-door-repairs-perth` homepage-grade AND keep the homepage
   itself strongly repair-focused (it's what Google already prefers to rank for us).
3. **edenroc's playbook is differentiated**: parts pages (garage door panels 590/KD14,
   panel replacement 170/KD6, roller/sectional parts KD 2–7), location pages, glass-doors niche
   (210/KD3), and *manuals/PDF resources* (Merlin 430R manual, Avanti remote instructions) that
   rank + earn links. Cheap wins we can copy with our motors/manuals content.
